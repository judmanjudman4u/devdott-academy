UI Design (User Interface Design):

UI design involves creating the visual elements and interactive components of a website or application that users interact with. It focuses on the layout, color scheme, typography, and overall aesthetic appeal to ensure a positive user experience. In this lesson, you will learn the principles and techniques of effective UI design.

BUX Design (User Experience Design) :

UX design focuses on creating products that provide meaningful and relevant experiences to users. This involves the design of the entire process of acquiring and integrating the product, including aspects of branding, design, usability, and function. In this lesson, you will learn the principles and techniques of effective UX design.

Web Design Principles and Prototyping :

Learn the fundamental principles of web design and how to create effective prototypes. This lesson will teach you how to plan, design, and test your web designs before implementation.
