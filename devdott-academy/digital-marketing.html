Introduction :

You will gain practical skills in Social Media Marketing, Google Ads, SEO, Content Marketing, Email Marketing, and E-commerce Marketing. This hands-on course is ideal for students, entrepreneurs, and business owners in Abuja who want to grow online, get digital jobs, or increase sales.

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) :

Learn how to improve website visibility and attract organic traffic through Search Engine Optimization (SEO). This practical training covers keyword research, on-page SEO, technical SEO, content optimization, link building, and Google Search Console. Develop the skills to help websites rank higher on search engines, reach the right audience, and generate more organic visitors for businesses.

social media and Content Marketing :

Learn how to create engaging content, manage social media platforms, and build a strong online presence for businesses and brands. This practical training covers social media strategy, content creation, graphic design basics, content planning, audience engagement, brand storytelling, and performance analysis. Gain the skills to attract followers, increase brand awareness, and use social media content to drive business growth.

E-commerce Marketing :

Learn how to attract customers, promote products, and increase sales for online businesses. This practical training covers e-commerce marketing strategies, social media advertising, product promotion, sales funnels, customer retention, conversion optimization, and analytics to help you grow a successful online store.

Email Marketing :

Learn how to plan, create, and manage effective email marketing campaigns that attract customers and grow businesses. Gain practical skills in building email lists, designing professional newsletters, writing engaging marketing emails, automating campaigns, and tracking performance to improve customer engagement and sales.
