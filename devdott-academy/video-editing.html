Video Editing Fundamentals :

Learn the foundations of video editing, from understanding editing software and timelines to cutting, trimming, arranging and combining video clips. This course introduces students to essential editing techniques and helps them develop the skills needed to create well-structured videos.

Learn how to enhance the audio quality of your videos, adjust colors to create the desired mood, and add visual effects to make your content more engaging. This course will teach you the techniques and tools needed for professional video post-production.
